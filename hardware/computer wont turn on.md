# Computer Won't Turn On

## Problem

The computer does not power on when the power button is pressed.

## Possible Causes

* Power cable is disconnected
* Faulty power outlet
* Power supply issue
* Battery is completely discharged
* Hardware failure

## Troubleshooting Steps

1. Check that the power cable is securely connected.
2. Test the power outlet with another device.
3. If using a laptop, connect the charger and allow it to charge.
4. Disconnect unnecessary peripherals and try powering on again.
5. Hold the power button for 10–15 seconds, then try again.
6. If the computer still does not power on, inspect the power supply or charger for possible failure.

## Resolution

Verify that the computer powers on normally after identifying and addressing the cause.
